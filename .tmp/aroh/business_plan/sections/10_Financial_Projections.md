# Financial Projections: Owner Intelligence That Pays for Itself From the First Quarter

> **Last Updated:** 2026-10-25
> **Project:** Aroh

## Executive Summary

Aroh's base case is built on one early milestone: ten free pilots by December 2026 and fifty paid contracts by March 2027. If that happens, the five-month pre-launch stub is a **loss of ₹2.70 Cr** — recognised revenue is only ₹0.78 Cr because the fifty customers are onboarded in March and ₹13.68 Cr of annual contracts is billed upfront but recognised over the following year. The business is profitable from FY2027–28 and reaches **₹95.76 Cr of revenue, ₹106.3 Cr of annual recurring revenue and ₹41.67 Cr of EBITDA in FY2029–30**, with 500 customers [[1]](#ref-1). DEWiN's ₹5 Cr bridges the pre-launch until the March payments arrive; after that, customer prepayments fund the business and cash ends FY2029–30 at ₹121.28 Cr, ₹65.19 Cr of which is customers' prepaid, not-yet-earned revenue.

Every figure below comes from the 14-sheet financial model, generated from the assumption register and checked against independent calculation scripts: revenue, gross profit and EBITDA agree within 0.5% in every period, the balance sheet balances to the rupee and cash never goes negative — in both the base and downside models [[1]](#ref-1), [[2]](#ref-2).

The plan's real risk is the milestone itself. Stress tests show that price, AI cost, churn, conversion and even a halved ramp are absorbed by the ₹5 Cr bridge — the constraint is the timing of the March receipts. Only the downside — no paid pre-launch customers and the slower previous plan (0 → 20 → 90 → 250) — needs outside money: about ₹6.0 Cr in total, which a ~₹5 Cr seed plus a gated agent-cloud budget covers (Section 09) [[3]](#ref-3).

### Key Insights

- Base case: revenue ₹0.78 / ₹25.38 / ₹52.59 / ₹95.76 Cr; EBITDA ₹−2.70 / ₹9.93 / ₹20.35 / ₹41.67 Cr; ₹106.3 Cr exit ARR [[1]](#ref-1).
- A ₹2.70 Cr loss in the five-month pre-launch stub, then profit from FY2027–28; DEWiN's ₹5 Cr bridges the pre-launch and customer prepayments fund the rest [[1]](#ref-1).
- The model ties to independent calculation scripts within 0.5% and balances in every period, base and downside [[2]](#ref-2).
- The decisive risk is the March 2027 milestone; the downside needs ~₹6.0 Cr, covered by a ~₹5 Cr seed [[3]](#ref-3).
- 500 customers is 6.0% of the base serviceable market — above the plan's 5% red-flag line (Section 02).

### Investment Implications

The projections describe Owner Intelligence as a business that earns its way from the second quarter of its first full year — if owners buy it as fast as the founders expect. That is a testable claim with a date on it: by 31 March 2027 Aroh either has about fifty paying customers or it does not. The plan is built so that investors price the company after that date, on evidence, while the existing owners carry the risk until then.
