# Aroh — Section 02 Sizing Method (pre-registered before data lands)

> **Written:** 2026-10-24, before Stage 1 research returned — so the method can't be bent to fit the numbers.

## Layer 3 — Bottom-up accounts × ACV (the anchor)

```
TAM accounts  = Indian businesses in the ₹100–₹5,000 Cr turnover band (all sectors)
SAM accounts  = TAM accounts × share in launch sectors (manufacturing, distribution & logistics, B2B services)
                 × digital-maturity filter (has ERP/CRM/email systems to watch)
TAM / SAM ₹   = accounts × recurring ACV
SOM           = Year-3 customers (Stage 2 scenario) × ACV;  penetration = SOM accounts ÷ SAM accounts
```

- ACV for sizing: use the S1 base ₹18L/yr (₹1.5L/month) as a placeholder until Stage 2 sets price; show ₹9L (₹75K/month) as the low case.
- Digital-maturity filter must be sourced (e.g., D&B / EY readiness data) or labelled LOW.

## Layer 1 — Cost of the problem (owner attention opportunity cost)

Only formulas built on verified figures. Candidate forms (pick after research):

- **Productivity form:** SAM accounts × average revenue × verified management/productivity uplift.
- **Time form:** SAM accounts × owner + senior-team hours on operational oversight × value of an hour.

Presented as "value at stake", never as revenue Aroh can capture.

## Layer 2 — Budget migration

```
Per account: (human CoS/EA/MIS salary cost) + (leadership AI copilot seats × price × 12) + (BI/MIS consulting spend)
Migration share: explicit assumption, labelled MEDIUM/LOW
```

Cross-check: Layer 2 per-account budget should be ≥ the Aroh ACV for the price to be credible.

## Triangulation rule

Show all three layers side by side. If Layer 3 SAM ₹ > Layer 2 total budget pool, flag it. If > 30% of the SAM figure rests on LOW-confidence inputs, stop and flag (A-020).

## Penetration check (AGENTS.md table)

Year-3 SOM ÷ SAM accounts: 0.5–1.5% realistic · 1.5–3% aggressive · > 5% red flag.
