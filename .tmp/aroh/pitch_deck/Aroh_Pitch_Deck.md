## Slide 3 — The enemy is the owner's head as the integration layer

- ERP, CRM, email, WhatsApp and managers all exist — and the owner joins them up, from memory, every day.
- Owners in an Indian field study worked **68 hours a week** and kept decisions in the family because they lacked the information to delegate.
- The real cost is not hours spent chasing. It is the **growth the owner never pursues** because they are busy checking that nothing broke.
- India's path to **$6T by 2030** (IMF) runs through firms that keep growing past the size one person can watch.

**Speaker notes:** Not a competitor — a behaviour. Nobody is blamed for checking personally, which is why it survives.

*Sources: Sections 00, 01 (Bloom et al., IMF)*
